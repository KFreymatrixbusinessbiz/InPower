WHEN CAPABILITY MOVES INSIDE

Most organizations think operating models change when technology changes.They change when capability moves.

Capability once lived outside the operation.With the technician.With the dealer.With the service organization.Now capability can live inside the operation.

The interruption did not change.The location of capability did.

Three behaviors become possible.

Recovery begins at the point of interruption.

The operation no longer waits for outside availability before first response.

Knowledge compounds inside the organization.

Each recovery strengthens internal capability instead of sending learning back outside.

Control moves to the operation.

The organization decides when recovery begins because capability is present where work happens.

The operating model changed because capability changed.Recovery could begin somewhere new.

Ownership changes accountability.
