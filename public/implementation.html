InPower turns inside-out recovery into an operating capability.

The objective is not to eliminate support. It is to stop making every interruption dependent on it.

The operating problem

Most service models begin after capability has already left the organization.

An interruption becomes a call, a queue, a dispatch, and a wait. InPower redesigns the first response so trained people inside the operation can begin safe recovery immediately.

The implementation system

Five parts work together.

  1. 01
    Understand the operation

    Map locations, workflows, security boundaries, response requirements, internal capability, and the real cost of waiting.

  2. 02
    Select for the outcome

    Match the environment to resilient platforms whose engineering supports the required recovery, volume, security, and continuity.

  3. 03
    Transfer factory knowledge

    Designated personnel receive structured, platform-specific training, documentation, and access to approved recovery information.

  4. 04
    Recover where work happens

    The team uses panel guidance, known procedures, and available parts to resolve common conditions without waiting for a dispatch.

  5. 05
    Escalate by condition

    Specialized support remains available. Escalation begins when the condition exceeds the safe internal recovery boundary.

Technology follows the operating requirement

InPower is not tied to one device architecture.

Matrix currently implements the model with Epson PrecisionCore, Brother Workhorse and Titan, and selected Kyocera modular platforms. The recovery path differs by platform; the governing principle does not.

The capability boundary

Your employees do not become copier technicians.

Moves inside

First response, approved recovery, routine replacement procedures, operating knowledge, and the decision to escalate.

Remains specialized

Conditions requiring advanced diagnosis, restricted procedures, specialized tooling, or support beyond the trained boundary.

The program structure

Capability is the service.

InPower operates 24/7 because the first response is available on the organization's schedule, not a dispatch schedule.

There is no separate InPower program fee. Supplies and eligible replacement parts are purchased through Matrix Business Systems, which maintains the implementation relationship and recovery resources.
