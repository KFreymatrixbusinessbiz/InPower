INSIDE–OUT

Most organizations recover from the outside.Few realize they have another choice.

Recovery does not begin when someone arrives.It begins where capability already exists.

The Traditional Model

For decades, this became the accepted operating model.

Not because it was right.

Because it was familiar.

Operational dependency is rarely questioned.It becomes ordinary.

The INSIDE–OUT Model

Nothing changed about the interruption.Everything changed about where recovery began.

The operating model changed before the technology did.

Three consequences.

Observation 01

Dependency becomes optional.

The organization is no longer designed around waiting for outside recovery.

Observation 02

Recovery begins immediately.

The first response can begin where the interruption occurs.

Observation 03

Capability remains with the organization.

The operating model retains knowledge instead of exporting recovery.

INSIDE–OUT is not a support strategy.It is an operating model.

Capability changes more than response time.
